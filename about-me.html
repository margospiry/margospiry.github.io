Some info about myself

Hello there, I'm Margo Spiry - a graphic and UX designer based in London. I am driven by the challenge of transforming complex problems into elegant design solutions, whether that is using Figma or Adobe. I have always been somewhat of the artist type, pencil in one hand and sketch book in the other, but my journey into design began after attending a lecture on visual perception.

Unsurprisingly, I studied Psychology as an undergraduate and am currently pursuing a master's degree in Human Computer Interaction. Career wise I have had all sorts of endevours from working as part of the UX Research Team at Playstation Studios to being a Radio Show Host. I have also dabbled in DJing, but that's neither here nor there.

When I'm not at my desk, I like to explore different creative outlets such as painting, photography and music. Creativity fuels pretty much every part of my life and I'm constantly looking for ways to infuse novel perspectives into my work.
